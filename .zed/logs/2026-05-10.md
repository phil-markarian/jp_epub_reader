## cargo deny tuning

Initial check failed on advisories + bans + licenses (all from Tauri's
transitive deps). Resolved:

- Allowlisted `BSL-1.0`, `CDLA-Permissive-2.0` in addition to the GPL +
  MIT/BSD/Apache/etc. set.
- `advisories.unmaintained = "workspace"` — only flag unmaintained
  crates we actually depend on directly. Tauri pulls in GTK3 + a
  handful of unic-* crates we can't do anything about.
- `bans.allow-wildcard-paths = true` + `publish = false` workspace-wide
  so intra-workspace path deps don't trip the wildcard rule.

`cargo deny check` now: advisories ok, bans ok, licenses ok, sources ok.
